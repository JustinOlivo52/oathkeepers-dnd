Terrain preview only. Blue squares mark party spawns and red squares mark enemy spawns. Forest trunks block movement and sight; brambles are difficult terrain. Alternate 2×2 spawn slots pass geometry checks, but Large-creature combat support remains pending.

Review limitations

Right-facing sprites are mirrored horizontally; north/south directions and extra attack sets remain future work. Watch for changing foot spacing, blade alignment, and occlusion when reviewing motion. The spider's far legs overlap in some poses; the ogre's club is hidden in two walking poses and its slam/recovery need more contrast. Terrain edges are not mathematically seamless. The forest scene uses stronger compression to remain under 400 KB. The tavern is prepared for guests rather than populated with foreground patrons.

Reviewed artwork may be cataloged before its rules exist. These pilot entries remain integration-pending. Asset selection must never make them playable solely because their PNGs load.
